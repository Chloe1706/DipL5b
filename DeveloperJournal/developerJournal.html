December 2025

I have been finding this p5.js project incredibly challenging! I am eager to learn more; however, I have found it increasingly difficult to comprehend code in this context. For instance, understanding and recreating loops has been difficult. I can recognise a loop when I see one but recreating it for my own purposes has not been particularly intuitive for me. That being said, I am trying to persevere with it because the potential for this medium really excites me.

I have enjoyed the research aspect of this project a lot. Given that I come from Fine Art, contextual research is fundamental to the study of art as it allows you to look at it through a more informed lens and notice choices that the artist made based on their background, beliefs and influences. I have thoroughly enjoyed bringing this aspect of my BA into this diploma. So much so, it has inspired me to base my p5.js studies on more traditional artworks.

February 2026

Starting the AR project was initially challenging. I found it difficult to navigate Unity’s complex UI with endless menus. That being said, as I am approaching the mid-point of this short project, I am starting to familiarise myself with certain processes.

I went back and forth on my idea for this project a lot. I knew that I wanted to integrate playing cards into image tracking AR but for a long time, I was unsure about what I wanted to make. Ultimately, it was the limitations of the chosen marker that would lead me to my final decision. I’ve learnt that AR image detection is not very compatible with symmetric markers with lots of white space and so as I began working on this project, I quickly learnt that standard playing cards were not suitable. Fortunately, I had a set of Bicycle: Gypsy Witch Playing cards which integrate Lenormand-style cartomancy with a standard deck. This makes them more varied and detailed by design and thus, suitable for AR image tracking. As I pivoted to using this deck, I ultimately decided to base my idea off Cartomancy single card readings.

March 2026

In recent weeks we have been working on the visual coding/Touch Designer project. As mentioned in my supporting documentation I followed the exercises provided on the VLE and then started looking at tutorials on youtube. I am genuinely excited by the creative possibilities afforded by Touch Designer. As we started this project I happened to borrow a Boss 505 loop station from a friend. As a result of this, In this project I have mostly been focussed on syncing visuals with live audio data from the loop station by plugging it into Touch Designer using an audio device in CHOP. As we only have a limited amount of time for this brief I have elected to make this my primary focus however, I would also be interested in exploring movement detection with Touch Designer in the future.

I think that this could be useful as I go into my third and final year of Fine Art BA. Historically, I’ve done quite a lot of work with projection and I think that touch designer will enable me to take my creative practice further and explore the possibilities of audience interaction with digital work.

April 2026

As part of the final project, we need to consider how our Interactive Experience and use of creative technology can be applied from an employability and industry perspective. At the time of starting this project I was already considering how I would be interested in pursuing a role within a multimedia experience company. It is my belief that as technology continues to develop there will be an increasing amount of opportunities within this sector. This idea was further solidified by the guest lecturer Duncan Fewkes who currently works for Holovis, a company that designs attractions utilising immersive technology, as well as the recent trip to Frameless and Vikings: The Immersive Experience in London. These experiences reaffirmed to me that there are increasing opportunities within this field. Furthermore, it is my intention with the Interactive Experience project as well as future work in my third year of study in Fine Art to develop a portfolio of work which speaks to this industry so that when I graduate, I am better equipped to pursue a role within this sector.

In my second year of study in Fine Art I wrote a lot about accessibility and art. This motivated my interest in the Creative Computing diploma as I believe that the integration of immersive and interactive technology within experiences may open doors to increased accessibility for a wider audience. This was one of the key factors I considered when designing the project for Interactive Experience. I think that work which utilises technology can be intricate and complex or immediate and intuitive. I am trying to focus more on the latter.
